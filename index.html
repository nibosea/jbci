Methodology

📐 Index Definitions

BCR (Beard Coverage Ratio) is the beard-covered share of the lower facial region, bounded by the infraorbital margin above and the mandibular line below. TBA (Total Beard Area) additionally includes growth extending beyond the mandibular line, which is why TBA can grow faster than BCR — as observed in the +27% YoY reading at Cologne 2026.

BCR = ( Sbeard∩face ÷ Slower-face ) × 100    TBA = Sbeard (projected, cm²)

📷 Measurement Protocol

For each Major, frontal imagery is sampled from three sources: the official player portrait, stage walk-out footage, and post-match interview footage. Beard regions are extracted with our in-house segmentation pipeline (FHSeg v4) and the three readings are averaged into the event value. Scale calibration uses the known interpupillary distance to convert pixel area to cm².

🧪 Density Grading

S: ≥70% (Elite) · A: ≥65% (Mature) · B: ≥55% (Established) · C: <55% (Emerging). The "Championship Beard" designation requires an S-grade reading combined with a Major title — a pairing achieved only once in the history of the index.
